How many tiles do I need?

Calculate floor or wall tile quantities with a visible layout waste allowance and round up to full supplier cartons so you see exact tile counts and total boxes to order.

Quick answer: US floor: a 12 × 10 ft room (120 sq ft) with 12 × 12 in tiles and 10% waste needs 132 tiles, or 14 ten-tile boxes (140 tiles). Metric: a 4 × 3 m room (12 m²) with 30 × 30 cm tiles and 10% waste needs 147 tiles, or 15 ten-tile boxes (150 tiles).

Tile layout waste allowances and common tile coverage

Choose a higher waste percentage whenever tiles are laid on an angle, around fixtures, or in narrow alcoves.

Layout pattern or tile sizeRecommended wasteCoverage per tileNotes
Straight / stacked grid10%—Lowest cut waste; keep 5–10% intact spares

How the calculation works

Convert room dimensions and tile dimensions into matching area units, divide room area by single-tile area, multiply by one plus your layout waste percentage, and round up to whole tiles. Divide by pieces per carton and round up again to full boxes.

What this estimate does not cover

This is an area-plus-waste model, not a cut-by-cut layout drawing. Grout joint width, L-shaped niches, diagonal or herringbone perimeter triangles, and large-format tile breakage increase waste. Confirm exact square footage per box and match batch/caliber codes before installing.
