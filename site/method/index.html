Method and assumptions for how many tiles do i need

Tile and carton quantity calculator

Divide room length times width by tile length times width, with all areas expressed in the same units. Multiply by one plus your waste percentage and round up to whole tiles. Divide by tiles per carton and round up again to whole cartons.

Limits and interpretation

This is an area estimate, not a tile cutting layout. Diagonal patterns, grout joints, broken tiles, niches and small edge cuts affect waste. Choose the allowance for your layout and confirm carton coverage and tile dimensions with the supplier.

Units and rounding

Lengths convert using exactly 0.3048 metres per foot and 2.54 centimetres per inch. Area and volume conversions use the corresponding squared and cubed factors. Counts round up where whole items must be purchased; wallpaper drops per roll and fabric pieces across a width round down. Displayed values may be rounded, while calculations retain additional precision.

Compare inputs with the worked examples on the calculator page. A different material, layout or measurement can require different assumptions.
