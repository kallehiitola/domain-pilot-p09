Method and assumptions

Tile and carton quantity calculator equation

Tiles needed = ceiling((room area / single tile area) × (1 + waste / 100)); Boxes = ceiling(tiles needed / tiles per box); Purchased tiles = boxes × tiles per box.

Step-by-step logic

Convert room dimensions and tile dimensions into matching area units, divide room area by single-tile area, multiply by one plus your layout waste percentage, and round up to whole tiles. Divide by pieces per carton and round up again to full boxes.

Units and rounding

Room dimensions convert at 0.3048 m per foot and tile sizes at 2.54 cm per inch. Both tile count and whole carton count round up.

Limits and edge cases

This is an area-plus-waste model, not a cut-by-cut layout drawing. Grout joint width, L-shaped niches, diagonal or herringbone perimeter triangles, and large-format tile breakage increase waste. Confirm exact square footage per box and match batch/caliber codes before installing.
